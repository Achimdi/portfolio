Food ordering website

Twzee Foods

A mobile-first menu and WhatsApp ordering website for a Nigerian healthy-food business. Visitors pick sizes and extras and send a ready-written order on WhatsApp. Prices live in one file the owner can update.

The problem

Customers should be able to see every item and price, build an order and send it in under a minute, and the owner should be able to change the menu and prices without a developer.

Objectives

  • Show the full menu with photos, prices, sizes and extras on a phone.
  • Turn a basket into a ready-written WhatsApp message.
  • Let the owner change the menu and prices by editing one file.
  • Load quickly on mobile data.

My role

I designed and built the site: the layout and visual design, the menu and ordering flow, the photo pipeline and the hosting.

Main features

Menu by category

Photos, prices, sizes, combos and optional add-ons, with category chips to jump between sections.

Order basket

Delivery or pickup: a flat ₦1,500 delivery fee within Enugu City, free delivery on orders from ₦30,000, and a bar showing how close the order is.

Order on WhatsApp

"Send my order" opens WhatsApp with the order written out. Nothing is sent until the customer presses send.

Live opening hours

An "Open now" or "Closed" badge for Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm, always in Nigerian time.

Catering and trays

Event packs and a custom food tray, with a quick way to ask for a quote.

How it's built

Open any part for the detail.

Code

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step.

Menu data

One commented file, js/menu-data.js, holds every product: name, description, price or sizes (each size can have its own photo), extras, a required choice, "Ask us" items, and items hidden until they get a price.

Photos

A Python script (Pillow) makes 400, 800 and 1200 px WebP copies of every photo, plus 5:4 card versions that show the whole picture on a soft blurred background, so posters with text are never cropped.

Hosting

Cloudflare (static assets on Workers) with security headers, deployed from GitHub.

Challenges and solutions

Prices that change often

Everything the owner might change, from products and prices to contact details, lives in one documented file, so an update is a small edit rather than a code change.

Ordering without a payment system

The basket becomes a ready-written WhatsApp message. The customer reviews it and presses send, and the business confirms the total and timing in the chat.

Opening hours for visitors anywhere

The open or closed badge is always worked out in Nigerian time (UTC+1), whatever the visitor's own time zone.

Poster photos that crop badly

The photo script places each picture whole on a blurred background in a 5:4 card, so text on a poster is never cut off.

Outcome and status
